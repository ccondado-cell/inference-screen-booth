# Design record

The documents here are the working record, kept verbatim so that any claim on the screen can
be traced to a ruling and a measurement.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `2026-11-inference-booth-screen-copy-v1.md` | Locked copy: the fifteen-scene table, the word limits, the claims and their evidence, and what was rejected |
| `2026-11-inference-booth-screen-decisions.md` | Decision log: every scene ruled, with the options on the table, the ruling, and the exact geometry behind it |
| `2026-11-inference-booth-screen-v1.3-prompt.md` | The v1.3 change request as commissioned, with build tasks and acceptance checks |
| `addendum.md` | Implementation record, newest first: what shipped in each version, what was measured, what is still unverified |

Two conventions worth knowing before reading:

- The decisions log is chronological, so its headings keep the scene numbers the scenes had
  when they were ruled. The mantra scene was deleted at v1.3.5 and the loop went from sixteen
  scenes to fifteen: **old 13 through 16 are now 12 through 15.** The copy document and the
  build use the new numbers.
- Scene studies and comparison pages used during the design pass (the `preview/` folder) are
  working material and are not published here.
